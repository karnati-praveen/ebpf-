## Defensible paper positioning

Present Shardwise as a measured, reproducible implementation of adaptive contiguous-layer inference for small decoder models on CPU-accessible heterogeneous machines. Attribute optimization, replay, fault tolerance and cost-aware movement to their prior art. The candidate contribution is their combination with explicit replay accounting and a careful measurement study; **combining published components does not by itself establish a novel algorithm**. The repository's current evidence is insufficient for a first consumer-cluster recovery claim or an outbound-only joining claim.

Before submission, prioritize independent physical consumer machines with at least one heterogeneous pairing and one >2-node configuration; matched static balanced, static optimized, adaptive-no-gate and adaptive-gate comparisons; abrupt process and machine loss; long-history replay cost and generated-token preservation; Q=1 versus saturated concurrency; repeated runs with uncertainty and disclosed failures. Include a comparable optimized public system where practical, with matching model/precision/workload, and explain API/algorithm differences when direct reproduction is impossible. Gate-benefit experiments should intentionally include both profitable moves and short-remaining-work moves where replay cannot amortize.

These are proposed experiments, not journal-imposed device or repetition minima. Existing manuscript evidence (65 completed runs, 939 requests; mostly 2–3 repeats per main condition) must not be re-labelled a larger physical testbed.

## Limits requiring follow-up

The search is bounded to approximately twenty minutes with three parallel research agents. It is not exhaustive. Some abstracts are the only accessible evidence; **UNVERIFIED means not established, not absent**. The requested exact sentence for every individual recorded field is not complete: short verified quotations anchor selected claims, with locations for paraphrases; restricted sources need further full-text transcription. No blanket “no prior art” conclusion is warranted. Venue requirements and the absence of optimized baselines remain independent submission blockers.
