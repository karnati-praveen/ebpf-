# Edge and consumer prior-art audit, 6 October 2026

Read the existing manuscript, NOVELTY.md and references.bib before searching. This research slice includes 21 entries: 18 papers and 3 software projects. It is a bounded research pass, not a proof of exhaustive coverage. Primary sources are linked in each CSV row; DOI metadata comes from publisher-deposited Crossref records and ACL Anthology. arXiv IDs, versions and author lists were checked against abstract pages. Some final-paper technical evidence comes from the freely available preprint and is explicitly labelled. UNVERIFIED denotes incomplete access or insufficient support; NOT DEMONSTRATED means the inspected work did not establish that property, rather than proving it impossible.

The CSV retains at most 25 verbatim words per work, with section/page/table locators for the other paraphrased claims. Consequently it does **not** fulfill a literal demand for an exact sentence for every field. A publisher-full-text audit is still needed for the abstract-only entries and per-field quotation completeness. It is better to expose this limitation than fabricate sentences or numbers. Exact excerpts are labelled as excerpts rather than complete sentences.

## Important metadata and version corrections

- **EdgeShard is now a 2025 IEEE IoT Journal article**, DOI 10.1109/JIOT.2024.3524255, with **five** authors. The four-author arXiv v1 is not the final author list.
- **TPI-LLM is IEEE Transactions on Services Computing 2025**, DOI 10.1109/TSC.2025.3596892. ICLR2025 acceptance was not verified; do not copy aggregator labels. Its 70B/3.1GB figure is emulated eight-device evidence, separate from the real four-laptop experiment.
- **LinguaLinked final ACL2024 has five authors**, confirmed against ACL Anthology and final PDF. Crossref omits Simeng Liu and shortens Ian G. Harris, so the BibTeX overrides that incomplete deposit with publisher-authoritative ACL metadata.
- **MDI-LLM is LANMAN2025**, DOI 10.1109/LANMAN66415.2025.11154542, not merely a 2025 preprint.
- **Prima.cpp v3 is July2026 and ICLR2026**, with eleven authors and 674ms/token for 70B. Its 26tokens/s32B number uses speculative decoding. Scheduling on an idle task queue is not verified active-request migration.
- **LOIP arXiv2512.21835 v3 is September2026**, with eight authors/five Jetsons. Version1 was named LIME and used seven authors/four Jetsons with different speedups. Do not mix these versions.
- **Galaxy+ (TMC2025)** adds rescheduling and straggler claims absent from original Galaxy; the original is tensor+sequence parallel, not layer pipeline. Original scaling percentages concern a repeatedly executed single layer, not whole autoregressive generation.
- **LL-CEIoT appeared 24September2026**, DOI10.1109/TMC.2026.3737299. Its abstract is a newly published placement/batching threat; hardware and state-migration properties remain unverified.
- **ATSInfer is single-device hybrid CPU/GPU**, not multi-machine. Correct paper title: Automated Tensor Scheduling for Hybrid CPU-GPU LLM Inference on Consumer Devices.
- **Current exo now includes topology-aware tensor parallelism and RDMA**, so older comparisons characterizing it solely as simple layer PP are outdated. GPUStack 2.x also supersedes several old wiki backend limitations.

## Access gaps and follow-up priorities

Full technical access remained unavailable for Galaxy+, Hepti, LL-CEIoT and the ICSCA2025 paper. The CSV marks hardware, recovery mechanism, exact baselines and migration costs unverified instead of inferring them from titles or secondary surveys. Parallax's reported millisecond p99 latency needs metric clarification before comparison to whole-request completion. EASTER averages latency over100userinputs, whereas50samples are used for neuron importance scoring. Its128tokens are **input** length; do not describe its 58.00→7.62s result as generating128tokens. Petals' 14-server internet deployment and its same-host GPU failure study are distinct experiments.

Closest primary-source reference lists were inspected for EdgeShard, Kafetzis, MDI, Prima, Petals, Jupiter and Galaxy; this led to LinguaLinked, Hepti, Galaxy+ and EASTER. Parent audit covers HexGen, HexGen-2, PipeInfer, PipeEdge, E2LLM, FlowSpec and other migration papers separately. Software rows remain visibly separate from peer-reviewed/preprint evidence.
